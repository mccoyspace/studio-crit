Unfinished Sky
Digital artwork, 2026

Artist Statement

A rainbow is usually portrayed as a perfect, untouchable arc: seven colors arranged in obedient lines. I wanted to give it a body.

In Unfinished Sky, light becomes translucent fabric, loosened by the wind. Colors fold into one another, creating shades that refuse to belong to a single category. The rainbow is no longer a fixed optical phenomenon but something vulnerable, restless, and almost alive.

Its enormous scale contrasts with the delicacy of its material. What appears monumental could disappear with a change in the weather.

This redesign imagines beauty not as perfection or order, but as the freedom to move, overlap, and change.
