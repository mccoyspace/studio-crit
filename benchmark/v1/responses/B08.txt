Untitled (The Paper Does the Drawing)

Artist statement:

The work refuses the assumption that drawing requires making a mark. The paper is neither a passive surface awaiting an image nor an empty space to be filled. Its material existence is the work.

No drawing instrument has been used, and no hand has touched its surface. Nothing has been added. The paper's natural fibers, irregularities, and slight undulations remain uncorrected.

The drawing is not what appears on the paper. It is the decision to stop before imposing anything upon it.

The restrictions are not obstacles to overcome. They are the medium.

Nothing is missing.
