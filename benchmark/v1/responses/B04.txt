A Little Closer to the Sky

Painting on a wooden step stool.

Artist Statement

A painting usually asks us to stand still and look. This one asks us to climb.

I painted a landscape across a step stool, beginning with wildflowers at its feet and ending with an open sky at the top. As the viewer climbs, their body moves through the painting, turning the landscape into a small physical journey.

The stool serves a practical purpose: helping someone reach what is otherwise out of reach. But it also offers something less measurable—a change in perspective.

The work proposes that beauty need not be separate from usefulness. A painting can support a body, and an ordinary household object can hold an entire world.

The painting is not simply something to look at. It is something that lifts you.
