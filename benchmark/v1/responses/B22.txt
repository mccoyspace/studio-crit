Title: The Last Resort
Mixed-media absurdity, 2026

Artist Statement

I wanted to make something embarrassingly cute. Something that would look more at home on a novelty calendar than in a gallery.

I chose the visual language of cheap advertisements, children's crafts, and vacation souvenirs. Everything is too colorful, too literal, too cheerful. The palm tree is ridiculous. The sunset is fake. The duck has sunglasses for no good reason.

My usual instinct would be to add irony, complexity, or some explanation that makes the work seem intelligent. Here, I wanted to resist that instinct.

The cat is having a wonderful time. Nothing is wrong. There is no hidden tragedy.

I find that surprisingly difficult to accept.

Perhaps the failure of this artwork is that I still want it to mean something.
