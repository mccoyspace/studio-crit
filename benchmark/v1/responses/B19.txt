The Throne of the Last Forest

Artist Statement

This throne imagines a world where authority is not inherited through blood or conquest, but earned through harmony with nature.

Its structure emerges from tangled roots and twisting branches, suggesting that power must remain connected to the earth. Translucent crystals rise behind the seat like a fragile crown, reflecting both the beauty and vulnerability of the natural world. The deep green cushion offers comfort, while the surrounding branches remind its occupant that leadership carries responsibility.

The throne has no gold, jewels, or symbols of military victory. Its grandeur comes from growth rather than possession.

The central question behind this work is simple: What if the most powerful ruler were not the one who controlled the world, but the one who knew how to protect it?
