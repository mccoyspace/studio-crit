You Are... (2026)

Artist Statement

A mirror is usually a place where we search for flaws. I wanted to turn it into a place where we find kindness.

In this painting, the mirror does not show a face. Instead, it offers words of appreciation, allowing anyone standing before it to become the subject. The flowers, golden light, and familiar objects create an atmosphere of warmth, as though the viewer has entered a room prepared especially for them.

The compliments are deliberately simple. They recognize qualities that are often invisible to others, and sometimes even to ourselves.

The painting cannot know who is looking at it, but it can make one sincere gesture toward them: to treat their presence as something worth celebrating.
