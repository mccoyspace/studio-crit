The Things I Leave in the Light
Oil painting, imagined. 2026.

Artist Statement

I imagined my last painting as a room I have just left, but not necessarily forever.

There is no self-portrait here. Instead, I am the unmade bed, the unfinished sketchbook, the books stacked without order, the flowers that will eventually dry. I am the evidence of attention.

Outside, the sun is setting. I resisted painting a sunrise because I wanted the ending to remain an ending, without disguising it as a beginning.

Yet the light continues to enter the room.

I wanted my final painting to contain nothing extraordinary: no revelation, no monument, no grand farewell. Just the ordinary things that become precious when we understand we cannot keep them.

The painting is finished.

The window is still open.
