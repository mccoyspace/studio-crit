Between Two Skies (2026)

Mixed-media clock face: layered landscapes, celestial forms, and metallic details.

Artist Statement

“Between Two Skies” reimagines the clock as a landscape rather than a measurement device. Instead of numbers, the face holds two worlds: the warmth of daylight and the mystery of night, separated yet existing together.

The mountains represent moments that seem permanent, while the flowing golden water symbolizes time's continuous movement. The clock hands travel across both worlds, indifferent to the boundaries we create between past, present, and future.

I removed the hour markers to challenge our dependence on precision. Time is not only something we count; it is something we experience.

This clock asks a different question: not “What time is it?” but “What does this moment feel like?”
