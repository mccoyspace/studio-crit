Pieces of a Person
Modular ceramic sculpture, variable dimensions

Artist Statement

Pieces of a Person exists between assembly and disassembly. Its interlocking ceramic fragments suggest a human figure, yet the gaps prevent the body from ever becoming entirely complete. Each fragment is simultaneously an independent sculptural object and part of a larger form.

The scattered pieces are not debris or discarded material. They belong to the sculpture just as much as the assembled figure does. There is no definitive arrangement, and no final piece that could resolve its incompleteness.

The work considers identity as something continually constructed from memories, relationships, losses, and experiences. Wholeness is not presented as the absence of missing parts, but as the ability to exist with them.

The sculpture is complete precisely because it remains unfinished.
