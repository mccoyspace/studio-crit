Held Between (2026)
Stone, forged steel, brass wire

Artist Statement

Held Between explores balance not as a state of peace, but as an ongoing negotiation between opposing forces.

The stones suggest permanence and weight, while the slender steel and taut brass wires reveal how precarious that permanence can be. A seemingly impossible arrangement is sustained through relationships of pressure, resistance, and dependence.

The empty space within the curved steel is as important as the material itself. It gives tension a visible form, allowing the viewer to imagine the forces holding everything together.

I wanted to create a sculpture that appears simultaneously stable and moments away from collapse. Its stillness is not the absence of movement, but the consequence of forces temporarily agreeing.

Balance, here, is not certainty. It is the fragile achievement of remaining together.
